From the samavar, to a box

The kettle every kahwa begins in.

A samavar is the copper kettle Kashmiri kahwa is brewed in — coals in the middle, tea all around, steam at the top. We put everything that goes into it in a box, so the cup can be made anywhere.

The experience

Ten slow minutes. That is the gift.

Guests don't just buy a souvenir — they take home a ritual. A brew card in every box walks them through it.

  1. Wake the spices

    Simmer water with a few cardamom pods and a piece of cinnamon.

  2. Add the kahwa

    A spoon of the kahwa blend. Lower the heat and let it steep gently.

  3. Bloom the saffron

    Drop in a few strands and watch the cup turn gold.

  4. Sweeten and pour

    A little honey, then pour into the cup. Sip it warm, unhurried.

Good to know

Before you order.

Can the box carry only our branding?

Yes. For hotel and corporate orders the lid, jar labels, tag and card carry your name and logo. Our name does not need to appear on the front.

Which box should a hotel start with?

Most counters suit The Spice Box. The Pair works well as an in-room welcome, and The Samavar Box is the premium gift. You can mix all three in one order.

Will guests know how to make it?

Every box includes a brew card with simple steps, so anyone can make a proper cup of kahwa at home.
